How To Play

Stack cute animals as high as you can without letting any fall off!

1
Aim & Move: Drag horizontally or use keys ← → / A D.
2
Rotate: Tap screen, press R or ↑, or use the rotation buttons.
3
Drop: Tap DROP or press Space / ↓.
4
Win Condition: In Bot or Friend mode, the player who causes any animal to tumble off loses!
