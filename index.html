Candy Tower Defense is an action-packed strategic tower defense game where your mission is to defend the jelly candy heart against swarms of attacking sweets! Place towers with complementary abilities, upgrade them to Master Tier, and conquer challenging boss encounters.

Features

  • 8 unique confectionery towers with distinct fire rates, ranges, and special damage effects.
  • 5 enemy archetypes: Gummy (Normal), Peppermint (Fast Sprinter), Jawbreaker (Armored Tank), Sugar Bean (Swarm), and King Gummy (Boss).
  • Tactical targeting priorities: switch any tower to target First, Last, Strongest, or Weakest.
  • Deep progression with Level 5 Master Tier upgrades.
  • Foolproof audio engine with procedural sound synthesizer fallback.
  • Aegis Protocol Autonomous Commander AI Agent with mathematical utility optimization, micro-targeting, and real-time reasoning timeline.

How to Play

  • Choose a tower from the build menu at the bottom.
  • Click or tap any open green pasture spot to place your defense tower. Towers cannot be placed on the winding path.
  • Click "START WAVE" or press Space to summon the enemy wave.
  • Earn Sugar Coins by popping enemies and clearing waves. Use your earnings to upgrade or build more towers!
  • Click on any placed tower to inspect stats, level up, sell, or toggle targeting priority.
