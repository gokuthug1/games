How to Play Blackjack

1. The Goal

The main objective of Blackjack is to beat the dealer's hand total without exceeding 21 points.

If your hand goes over 21, you "Bust" and lose your bet immediately, regardless of what the dealer subsequently draws.

10 A
Perfect Natural Blackjack!

2. Card Values

Cards hold the following values:

  • 2 through 10: Face value (e.g., 5 is worth 5 points).
  • Jacks, Queens, Kings: Worth exactly 10 points.
  • Aces: Flexible! Worth either 1 or 11 points, dynamically choosing whichever value benefits your hand the most.
5 A = 16 (Soft)
5 A 10 = 16 (Hard)

3. Your Decisions

During your turn, you can choose from these actions:

  • Hit: Request another card to increase your score.
  • Stand: Lock in your score and end your turn.
  • Double Down: Double your original bet, receive exactly one more card, then stand.
  • Split: Separate a starting pair into two independent hands with matching bets.

4. Dealer Rules & Payouts

The dealer must play by strict mathematical rules:

  • The dealer must hit sequentially until they reach a minimum score of 17.
  • Standard player wins are paid 1 to 1. Natural Blackjacks pay a premium 3 to 2.

Note: You can configure whether the dealer stands or hits on a Soft 17 using the Settings menu.
